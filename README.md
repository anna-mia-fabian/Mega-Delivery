
# Mega Delivery | Project Management Case Study

**Project Planning • Product Management • Business Analysis**

## About the Project

Mega Delivery is a simulated educational project focused on planning and managing the development of a digital food delivery platform.

The case demonstrates practical approaches to project coordination, product requirements, resource estimation, budgeting, risk management, and team responsibilities.

**Project type:** Educational case study  
**Focus:** IT Project & Product Management  
**Portfolio by:** Anna Mia Fabian
